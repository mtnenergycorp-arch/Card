How this game scores
  • Everyone for themselves. Scores are net — the lowest handicap plays off scratch and everyone else off the difference; strokes fall on the hardest holes by stroke index (gold cells • dots).
  • Each hole scores by finish: 1st = 4 pts, 2nd = 3, 3rd = 2, 4th = 1 (5th+ = 0). Players tied for a place split those places' points evenly.
  • Bonuses (don't carry): a natural (gross) birdie is +1 to that player, and a greenie (tap the Greenie row to award) is +1 — awarded every hole regardless of a carry.
  • Tie for 1st → carry. If two or more share the low net, no finish points are awarded and the whole hole's finish points roll onto the next hole (greenie/birdie still score). The pot builds until someone wins 1st outright.
  • Money: every point is worth that hole's bet, and each point's winner collects it from every other player. Totals always net to zero across the group.
  • Presses: raise the Bet $ on a hole and it carries on; the back nine auto-presses to the hole-9 value + $1 (min $2).
