How High-Low scoring works
  • Six points are in play on every hole: Low (2) — lower best net · High (2) — lower worst net · Greenie (1) — tap the Greenie row to award · Net birdie (1) — a team’s best net is under par and the other’s isn’t.
  • Sweep = ×2. Win all six points outright and the hole doubles to 12 (shown gold).
  • Ties push (no points). Money = (Team 1 − Team 2 points) × that hole’s bet; Running $ is from Team 1’s perspective (positive = Team 1 up).
  • Presses. Raise the Bet $ on any hole and that higher stake carries through the rest of the round. There’s an automatic press on hole 10 (gold edge): the back nine plays for double the front-nine stake.
  • Strokes. A gold cell with a • dot means that player gets a stroke there (two dots = two strokes), allocated by the S.I. (stroke index) row from each player’s handicap. The small grey number is the net score.
  • A hole only counts once all four scores are entered.
