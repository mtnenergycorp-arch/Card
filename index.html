How High-Low scoring works
  • Six points are in play on every hole:
    • Low — 2 pts. Team with the lower best net score.
    • High — 2 pts. Team with the lower worst net score (lower of the two high scores).
    • Greenie — 1 pt. Awarded by hand in the Grn column (closest to the pin / on the green).
    • Net birdie — 1 pt. A team’s best net score is under par and the other team’s isn’t.
  • Sweep = ×2. Win all six points outright on a hole and the total doubles to 12.
  • Ties push. A tied Low, High, or net birdie scores nothing; an unawarded greenie scores nothing.
  • Money. Each point is worth that hole’s bet. The dollar move = (Team 1 points − Team 2 points) × bet. The Total column is the running balance from Team 1’s perspective (positive = Team 1 is up).
  • Net scoring. Strokes are given on the hardest holes by stroke index, from each player’s course handicap. A green net number means a stroke was received on that hole.
  • A hole only counts once all four scores are entered.
  • Result legend: L Low · H High · G Greenie · B net Birdie, where 1 = Team 1, 2 = Team 2, ½ = push, · = none.
