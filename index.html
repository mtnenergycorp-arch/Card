How High-Low scoring works
  • Teams. 2–6 players split into two teams. Each hole compares the teams’ best and worst net scores, so team sizes can be uneven.
  • Six points per hole: Low (2) — lower best net · High (2) — lower worst net · Greenie (1) — tap the Greenie row to award to a player (their team gets the point) · Net birdie (1) — a team’s best net is under par and the other’s isn’t.
  • Sweep = ×2. Win all six points outright and the hole doubles to 12 (shown gold).
  • Net — low man plays off scratch. The lowest handicap in the group plays off 0 and everyone else gets the difference (a 5 and a 10 become 0 and 5). Those strokes fall on the hardest holes by S.I., shown as a gold cell with • dots. The “→N” by a handicap is the strokes that player receives; the small grey number is the net score.
  • Presses. Raise the Bet $ on any hole and that higher stake carries to the end of the round. Automatic press on hole 10 (gold edge): from hole 10 the stake is the hole-9 value + $1 (the back nine is always worth at least $2 per point).
  • Money = (Team 1 − Team 2 points) × that hole’s bet; Running $ is from Team 1’s perspective (positive = Team 1 up). A hole counts once every player has a score.
